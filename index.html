01.What is Compostela?

Compostela a Catholic Press focused on living our lives in Christ. All of our material is concise, clear and founded in faith & tradition. For more info, visit - https://compostela.substack.com/p/what-is-compostela

02.God

Our one God in three divine persons, Father, Son & Holy Spirit is the One, True & Living God. The only way to Salvation is through Our Lord & Savior Jesus Christ.

03.Roman Catholicism

Jesus Christ, through St. Peter and His Apostles, founded the Holy Catholic Church for the preservation of Sacred Scripture, Tradition and Apostolic Succession as way to Christ for our Salvation and as a standard of being to rival Satan and his works.

04.The Saints

The Church and Tradition upholds and venerates individuals who exemplify Christ on Earth as people who we should look to for inspiration & ask them to pray for us to Christ in Heaven.

05.Tradition

"Tradition is not the worship of ashes, but the preservation of fire" - Gustav Mahler. Tradition is the antidote to Satanism.

06.Family

A strong marriage creates strong children, which creates a strong family to be resiliate and prosperous in a fallen world. A refuge from the storms.

07.Home

A house is a building, a home is the life inside of it. Our homes should reflect our values & ethics. Good design yields good thoughts and a good life.

09.Duties

The Lord granted us gifts in order that we can bring His light to our familes, communities & world. It would be disrepectful to not use those gifts to their fullest extent.
