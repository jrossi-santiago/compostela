The Storm on the Sea of Galilee

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1633

Rembrandt's only seascape, and his reading of Mark 4:35–41. Thirteen men fight the rigging of a boat driven up the face of a wave; a fourteenth sits at the stern, unhurried. The whole picture is built so the light falls where the storm is loudest. A refuge from the storms, hung on your wall.
